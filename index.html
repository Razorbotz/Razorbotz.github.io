Who Are We?

Arkansas Razorbotz is a student team at the University of Arkansas designing, building, and programming autonomous lunar mining rovers for the NASA Lunabotics Challenge at Kennedy Space Center.

Our Competition

Lunabotics is structured like a formal NASA mission. We don't just build a robot—we manage a full systems-engineering lifecycle. We submit design papers, present trade studies to a NASA review panel, and validate our mechanical and software systems under strict constraints.

The finals run over a week each May at NASA's Kennedy Space Center, testing our hardware in a specialized arena filled with highly abrasive lunar regolith simulant. A scoring run must excavate regolith, traverse an obstacle filled zone, then deposit the regolith and navigate back.

FAQ

What majors can join?

Most of our team studies mechanical, electrical, or computer engineering, or computer science—but operating complex rovers requires fabricators, designers, operations managers, and systems engineers just as much. Every major is welcome.

Do I need robotics experience?

No prior robotics experience is required. If you have experience, you can start contributing right away. If not, the team is developing onboarding and training materials.

What is the time commitment?

Meetings last an average of four hours a week, but students often work more than that, especially during the weeks leading up to competition.
